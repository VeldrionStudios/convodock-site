No messages are appearing

On Android, check that notification access is enabled for ConvoDock and that the original messaging app is allowed to show message notifications. ConvoDock cannot import old chat history.

Reply is unavailable

Direct reply works only when the original Android notification includes an active quick-reply action. If the action is missing or expired, open the original app.

Delete local data

Use ConvoDock's privacy controls to delete local messages, contacts and identity links. You can also clear app storage or uninstall the app from your device settings.

Disconnect notification access

Open Android Settings, go to Special app access, then Notification access, and turn off ConvoDock. This stops future notification capture.

iPhone and iPad

iOS and iPadOS do not provide ConvoDock with a general third-party messaging notification inbox. Do not expect Android-style notification capture on iPhone or iPad.

Purchases and restore

Purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple's App Store. If a purchase is pending, cancelled or refunded, ConvoDock will reflect the store status when available. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling or changing devices.
