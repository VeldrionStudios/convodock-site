One inbox, clear limits.

ConvoDock is designed around what the platform genuinely allows. It handles supported Android notifications carefully and keeps users in control of sensitive data.

01

Notification-derived inbox

Messages appear after Android notification access is enabled and supported notifications arrive.

02

Reply when supported

Replies use the original notification quick-reply action when one is still available.

03

Stored on device

Captured content and local contacts are stored on your device by default.

04

No cloud account required

ConvoDock does not require a cloud account for the local inbox experience.
