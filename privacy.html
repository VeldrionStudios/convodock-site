ConvoDock Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Overview

ConvoDock is an Android, local-first inbox for notification-derived messages. It does not require a cloud account for the local inbox and does not upload your contacts or messages to a ConvoDock server.

ConvoDock is published by Veldrion Studios. For privacy questions, deletion help or rights requests, contact veldrionstudios@gmail.com.

What ConvoDock processes

ConvoDock may process the following data on your device:

  • Notification content and metadata from source apps you choose to capture after enabling Android notification access.
  • Sender names, conversation titles, message previews or message text, timestamps, source app names, package names, notification identifiers, notification removal events and available quick-reply actions.
  • Sender icons, notification image previews, attachment metadata or attachment media URIs only where Android notifications legitimately expose them.
  • Files, photos or links you choose to import manually through Android's share sheet.
  • Contacts you manually create, contacts you choose to import from your phone contacts, custom contact photos copied into app-private storage, initials, favourites and linked service identities.
  • Per-app and per-contact capture preferences, unread counts, search/filter state, theme choice and privacy settings.
  • Local entitlement state for a free trial or lifetime unlock, and Google Play purchase verification records where needed for purchase, restoration, refund or revocation handling.

Android notification access

Android notification access is sensitive. If you enable it, ConvoDock can read notification content and metadata visible to Android from supported source apps. Notifications can contain private messages, one-time codes, financial alerts, health information or other sensitive information.

ConvoDock uses notification access only to build the local inbox, update notification-derived message state and send supported quick replies through Android notification reply actions when an active source notification provides one.

  • ConvoDock starts capturing only after notification access is granted.
  • ConvoDock can only read content exposed by notifications; it does not retrieve complete message history.
  • ConvoDock may make a best-effort attempt to copy a notification-exposed attachment or media URI into local encrypted storage. If Android or the source app does not grant access, ConvoDock stores an unavailable-attachment placeholder and offers to open the original app.
  • ConvoDock attempts to filter likely one-time passcodes and verification-code notifications before storing them, but no automated filter can identify every sensitive notification.
  • If the original app hides notification content, ConvoDock may store only that the content was hidden or unavailable.
  • ConvoDock does not request SMS database access, call-log access or accessibility access for message scraping.
  • ConvoDock does not use unofficial messaging APIs, reverse-engineered private endpoints or automated credential collection.
  • Quick replies are sent only through an available Android notification reply action. ConvoDock does not claim delivery, read status or end-to-end message history.

Source app choices and duplicate alerts

ConvoDock includes per-app capture controls. You can pause capture for a supported source app, and ConvoDock will ignore new notifications from that app while paused. Existing local messages remain until you delete them.

ConvoDock can show its own notifications for captured messages if you allow ConvoDock notifications in Android. To reduce duplicate alerts, ConvoDock may guide you to Android notification settings for each source app and say: "now turn off source app pop-ups if you want only ConvoDock alerts." This is optional. ConvoDock does not silently change another app's lock-screen, badge, sound, vibration or pop-up settings.

Manual share imports

If you choose ConvoDock from Android's share sheet, ConvoDock may import the shared text, link, photo or file into a local "Shared to ConvoDock" conversation. Manual share imports are initiated by you and are not automatic source-app capture.

Shared files are copied into ConvoDock's local app storage where possible and encrypted with an Android Keystore-protected key. If the shared item cannot be copied, ConvoDock may store only that an attachment was shared and unavailable.

Contacts, identity links and photos

You can use ConvoDock without importing phone contacts. If you choose to import contacts, ConvoDock uses contact permission only to help organise the local inbox and link identities. You can manually link or unlink source-app identities, pause a linked identity for a contact, rename contacts and choose contact photos.

Where a notification exposes a sender image or icon, ConvoDock may use it locally as a suggested or fallback photo. Source apps do not always expose profile photos, and ConvoDock does not scrape source apps to fetch them.

Revoking contact permission stops future contact import access but does not automatically delete contacts already stored in ConvoDock. Use ConvoDock's delete controls, clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove local data.

Storage and security

Captured messages, linked identities and sensitive metadata are stored locally on your device in an encrypted database. The database key is protected with platform secure storage. Copied notification attachments and user-shared attachments are stored locally in encrypted app-private files using an Android Keystore-protected key. Custom contact photos are copied into ConvoDock app-private storage. Queued native notification events are stored in protected app storage and are removed after successful processing to minimise retention.

No local device storage can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, backups, operating-system vulnerabilities or compromised device credentials may be able to access app data.

Sharing and servers

ConvoDock does not sell personal data. ConvoDock does not upload notification-derived messages, attachments, imported contacts or linked identities to a ConvoDock server.

The current Android inbox does not use a ConvoDock cloud account, cross-device sync, in-app calling, email inbox aggregation, calendar/reminder integration, analytics SDK or advertising network.

If you buy or restore the lifetime unlock, ConvoDock may send Google Play purchase verification data to a Veldrion Studios purchase-verification backend. This backend is used only to check the purchase with the Google Play Developer API, acknowledge valid purchases, restore access, bind a purchase token to a hashed local ConvoDock installation identifier, and recognise cancelled, refunded or revoked purchases. It does not receive your messages, notification contents, contacts, contact photos or attachments.

If optional cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, email/calendar integrations or account features are added later, this policy and the in-app disclosures should be updated before those features are released.

Purchases

The Android lifetime unlock is processed by Google Play. Google may process payment details under its own terms. ConvoDock does not receive your full payment card details.

ConvoDock offers a genuinely free seven-day local trial that does not ask for payment details, does not auto-renew and does not automatically charge when it expires. After the trial, you may choose whether to buy the optional lifetime unlock. The intended lifetime price is GBP 4.99, with Google Play supplying the final displayed local price.

To verify purchases, ConvoDock may process product identifiers, package name, purchase status, transaction identifiers, purchase tokens, a hashed random app-installation identifier, hashed purchase-token records and hashed order identifiers needed to unlock access, acknowledge valid purchases, handle pending purchases, restore purchases, recognise cancellations, revoke refunded purchases and reduce token replay abuse. Purchase tokens are not embedded as app secrets and should not be stored in public repositories.

Support and website visits

If you contact support, ConvoDock may process your email address, device/app details and information you choose to include in the support request. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to respond, maintain records and protect the service unless a longer period is required by law.

The ConvoDock website may be hosted by a third-party hosting provider that processes standard technical logs such as IP address, browser details and request times for security and reliability.

Retention and deletion

ConvoDock keeps locally captured messages, copied attachments, shared imports, contacts, identity links, copied contact photos and settings on your device until you delete them, use the in-app delete-all-data control, clear app storage or uninstall the app.

Disconnecting notification access or pausing an app stops future capture but does not automatically delete existing local records. Successfully processed queued notification events are removed from the native queue to minimise retention.

Purchase verification records, including hashed purchase-token and app-installation records, may be retained by Veldrion Studios and Google for support, accounting, fraud prevention, restoration and legal compliance purposes. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to respond, maintain records and protect the service unless a longer period is required by law.

Your controls

  • Grant or revoke Android notification access.
  • Allow or block ConvoDock's own notifications.
  • Choose which source apps ConvoDock captures from.
  • Turn off source app pop-ups yourself in Android settings if you want only ConvoDock alerts.
  • Share files, photos or links into ConvoDock manually through Android's share sheet.
  • Import contacts or use ConvoDock without contact import.
  • Link, unlink or pause source-app identities for a contact.
  • Delete all ConvoDock local data.
  • Open the original source app instead of replying in ConvoDock.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, object to certain processing, or complain to a data-protection authority. Much of ConvoDock's inbox data is stored only on your device, so you can delete it directly in the app or by clearing app storage.

For support records or store-related records available to Veldrion Studios, contact veldrionstudios@gmail.com. If UK data-protection law applies, you can contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/.

Children

ConvoDock is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used by children where parental consent is required by applicable law.

Changes

ConvoDock may update this policy when the app changes. Material privacy changes should be reflected before the related app update is released.
